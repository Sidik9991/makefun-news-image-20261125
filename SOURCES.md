# Sources — The World in One Day, Wednesday 25 November 2026

Every story in entry.png happened, or was first reported, on
25 November 2026 in some time zone. Research was done on the morning
of 25 November (UTC) from wire and broadsheet reporting dated that
day. Times are UTC report/event times where a source gives one.

## The 15 pictured stories

1. **Shenzhou-24 lifts off** — Long March 2F/G from Jiuquan at
   00:18:52 UTC; crew Tang Hongbo, Wu Yi and Muhammad Zeeshan Ali,
   the first foreign astronaut to visit Tiangong. Reuters, Xinhua,
   Wikipedia mission log (launch "successful", in orbit by 01:15).
2. **Asia stocks slide** — Reuters, early Wednesday: MSCI Asia-Pacific
   ex-Japan −2.3%, South Korea −5.3%, Taiwan −5.1%, Nikkei −2.9% as
   fresh US tariff threats hit chip-heavy markets; Nvidia −5.9%.
3. **Australia–Solomon Islands security pact** — Reuters, Sydney,
   Nov 25: Canberra becomes the Solomons' defence partner of choice;
   the earlier China pact is reported scrapped. Guardian live, 00:43.
4. **Russian drone enters Polish airspace** — Pollar/Brussels wire:
   a Russian drone briefly entered Polish airspace during an
   overnight attack on Ukraine, days after Kh-101 missiles came down
   in Poland; Chancellor Merz vows Germany's "complete support".
5. **Kyiv-region tower strike, toll 11** — RFE/RL: rescuers pulled a
   third body from a drone-hit apartment block near Kyiv, bringing
   the death toll to 11.
6. **Ukraine sinks Russian warship off Benghazi** — President
   Zelenskyy confirmed Ukrainian forces struck a Russian warship and
   a cargo vessel near Benghazi, Libya, sinking the warship. RFE/RL.
7. **Shanghai party chief under probe** — Reuters, Nov 25: Politburo
   member Chen Jining is under anti-graft investigation; Shanghai's
   party leadership pledged loyalty to Beijing in Jiefang Daily.
8. **WHO evacuates Ebola hospital, DR Congo** — WHO Director-General
   Tedros: health workers evacuated from a hospital in Akwe, Ituri,
   after sheltering three days from fierce fighting; the outbreak
   stands at 11,762 cases and 5,775 deaths. Reported Nov 25 (ICT).
9. **Baghdad moves on Kurdish oilfields** — Starlit Daily, Nov 25:
   Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi directed federal units to seize
   oilfields and crossings in Kurdish-run regions.
10. **Car bomb in Colombia injures 23** — Starlit Daily, Nov 25:
    a car bomb injured at least 23 people.
11. **Seoul Metro strike called off** — Korea Times, 00:37 UTC:
    labour and management reached a last-minute wage deal, averting
    a planned general strike.
12. **Fiji declares nationwide disaster** — wire roundup, Nov 25:
    Fiji's government declared a nationwide disaster amid an
    El Niño dry spell.
13. **England level the T20 series** — school-assembly sports wire,
    Nov 25: England beat Australia by eight wickets in the second
    T20I, chasing 185 to level the five-match series 1–1; Jake
    Fraser-McGurk made 27 off 13 balls.
14. **Cubs sign Teruaki Sato** — ESPN, 00:38 UTC: the Chicago Cubs
    agreed a six-year, $120M deal with the two-time Japanese MVP.
15. **72 orangutans rescued** — Bernama roundup via The Capital
    Post, Nov 25: BKSDA and YIARI have rescued 72 orangutans from
    wildfires in West Kalimantan since August.
